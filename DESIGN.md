## Brand & Style

This design system crafts an evocative, editorial canvas for high-end wedding and portrait photographers. The aesthetic draws heavily from luxury European print editorial, cinematic film stills, and fine-art monographs. It is rooted in intentional restraint: ample whitespace, graceful transitions, delicate hairlines, and quiet typographic authority.

### Personality & Emotional Resonance
- **Romantic & Intimate:** Soft, humanized interactions evoke reverence, memory, and emotional permanence.
- **Timeless & Refined:** Free from ephemeral UI trends, relying on classical proportions, deliberate pacing, and archival balance.
- **Cinematic & Story-Driven:** Interfaces behave like curated gallery exhibits or art books, prioritizing full-bleed visual rhythm and quiet typographic annotations.

### Design Movement
**Minimalist Editorial with Tactile Warmth:** Merging the architectural rigor of bespoke print magazines with digital fluidity. The system avoids loud flat vectors, synthetic high-saturated gradients, or aggressive rounded buttons, adopting instead whisper-thin borders, layered warm-linen panels, and bespoke golden micro-details.

## Colors

The palette reproduces physical fine-art paper stocks, darkroom tones, and warm afternoon light. The primary canvas is warm and tactile rather than sterile #FFFFFF.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary Canvas (`#FAF8F5` to `#F5F1EB`):** Archival ivory and raw linen. Used for structural backdrops, elevated card backgrounds, and framing borders.
- **Deep Espresso & Charcoal (`#1E1B18` & `#2C2825`):** High-density dark earth tones replacing harsh stark blacks for headlines, body copy, and high-emphasis controls.
- **Champagne Gold (`#B39268`):** The signature accent. Used sparingly for curated accents, badge highlights, active states, delicate dividers, and award labels.
- **Rose Taupe & Sand (`#D9C7B8` & `#E5DFD7`):** Tonal bridge colors for delicate hair-thin outlines, understated hover washes, and secondary surfaces.

### Usage Hierarchy
- Primary text should never fall below contrast ratios against ivory backdrops; use `#1E1B18` for copy under 18px.
- Use the champagne accent (`#B39268`) with precision: no more than one focal accent per viewport tier to preserve high-luxury pedigree.

## Typography

Typography establishes an editorial balance between classical literature and modern gallery catalogs. 

### Typographic Principles
- **Headings (Playfair Display):** Set with relaxed, elegant leading. Italic cuts are deployed intentionally for conversational sub-clauses, romantic quotes, or chapter-style section headers (`title-editorial`).
- **Body & Controls (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Selected for its warm, geometric clarity and open counters. Rendered primarily in light (`300`) and regular (`400`) weights to preserve an airy, unobtrusive cadence.
- **Eyebrows & Metadata (`label-caps`):** Generously tracked uppercase strings (+18% letter spacing) evoke luxury perfumery and couture invitations. Always pair with short, definitive phrases (e.g., "INVESTMENT & COLLECTIONS", "PROVENCE, FRANCE").

## Layout & Spacing

Whitespace functions as an active compositional tool, creating contemplation and elevating imagery into fine art.

### Grid & Composition
- **12-Column Asymmetric Fluid Grid:** Designed for varied editorial layouts: alternating 7/5 splits, off-center 4/8 photo-essay sequences, and full 12-column panoramic spreads.
- **Desktop Margins:** Generous 4rem margins preserve an expansive border around the experience, resembling a gallery matboard.
- **Breathing Space (`space-2xl`):** Major narrative chapters (e.g., transitioning from Portfolio highlights to Investment Tiers) must be separated by at least 7rem of whitespace.

### Responsive Behavior
- **Mobile (< 768px):** Collapses into a 4-column column layout with 1.5rem side gutters. Full-bleed vertical image framing takes precedence over horizontal pairing.
- **Desktop (≥ 1024px):** Dual-height staggering and horizontal breathing room mirror the experience of turning coffee table book pages.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is delicate, avoiding heavy digital drop shadows in favor of tonal stepping and soft, organic light falloff.

### Techniques for Hierarchy
- **Tonal Stepping:** Surfaces elevate by shifting from ivory (`#FAF8F5`) to warm linen-rose (`#F5F1EB`), framed by 1px hairlines tinted with `#E5DFD7`.
- **Filmic Ambient Shadows:** When an element floats (e.g., booking drawer, sticky navigation, lightbox view), the shadow must be diffused, tinted with warm umber rather than neutral grey:
  - *Filmic Soft:* `0 12px 36px -8px rgba(44, 40, 37, 0.06), 0 2px 6px 0 rgba(44, 40, 37, 0.02)`
  - *Modal Focus:* `0 24px 64px -12px rgba(30, 27, 24, 0.12), 0 4px 12px 0 rgba(30, 27, 24, 0.04)`
- **Image Overlays & Backing Mats:** Imagery frequently sits inside a 6px to 12px warm linen matboard border (`#FAF8F5`) before sitting onto secondary canvas backdrops.

## Shapes

The shape system adopts a soft, architectural geometry (`roundedness: 1`). Corners are gently shaved (4px for inputs/chips, 8px for containers/cards) to soften digital harshness without dissolving into generic, overly bubbly capsules.

### Shape Philosophy
- **Crisp Structural Containers:** Main portfolio frames, inquiry sheets, and package modules utilize 4px–8px radius, echoing fine-art printing paper edges.
- **Circular Seals:** Reserved strictly for circular seal badges (e.g., "Featured in Vogue", "Limited 2025/2026 Dates") and floating audio or gallery play controls.
- **Dividers & Framing:** Lines remain ultra-thin (`1px`) utilizing muted sand hues (`#E5DFD7`).

## Components

### Buttons & Action Links
- **Primary Button:** Filled with deep charcoal `#2C2825`, text in warm ivory `#FAF8F5`, 4px border-radius. Letter-spaced uppercase text (`label-caps`). Subtle hover lift with a deep espresso transition (`#1E1B18`).
- **Editorial Text Button:** Underlined text button with a 1px border separated by 4px baseline offset in `#B39268`. Transitions smoothly to `#1E1B18` on hover.
- **Secondary Button:** 1px hairline border in `#2C2825` with transparent background, hover shifting to soft taupe surface wash (`#F5F1EB`).

### Form & Inquiry Fields
- **Editorial Inputs:** Minimalist single bottom-border (`1px solid #E5DFD7`) that transitions to champagne gold (`#B39268`) on focus. Floating italic labels in `Playfair Display`.
- **Date & Location Selectors:** Subtle card-like interactive selectors with micro-uppercase badges indicating wedding season availability.

### Selection Controls (Checkboxes & Radios)
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Tailored 1px outline boxes and circles in `#B39268` filled with `#FAF8F5`. Active state displays a delicate, sharp-edged gold pip or check.

### Package & Investment Tiers
- **Pricing Cards:** Tall, book-proportioned modules. Signature collection features a warm rose-linen background (`#F5F1EB`), subtle `#B39268` border highlight, an italic serif headline, and an itemized breakdown set with generous leading.

### Testimonial & Story Modules
- **Editorial Pull-Quotes:** Large centered or off-center Playfair Display italic text with faint champagne quotation marks. Attribution formatted with a thin 32px gold divider line and uppercase subtext.

### Lightbox & Gallery Frames
- **Image Displays:** Subtle filmic aspect ratio frames (4:5, 3:2, 16:9). Captions sit below images in small caps `label-caption`, indicating location and camera format (e.g., "Lake Como, Italy — 35mm Archival Film").
